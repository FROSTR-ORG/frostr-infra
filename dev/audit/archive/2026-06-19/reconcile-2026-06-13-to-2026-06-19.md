(`igloo-ui`, `igloo-shared`, `igloo-pwa`, `igloo-chrome`, `igloo-home`) against

and a long tail of dedup / magic-value / adversarial-test gaps. The original pass
was symptom-accurate but shallow on semantic seams — noted per target below.
